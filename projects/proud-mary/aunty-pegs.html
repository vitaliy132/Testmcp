AUNTY PEG'S

Monday – Sunday 9am to 5pm. Aunty Peg's is Proud Mary's roasting home — visit for wholesale pickups, merch, and the smell of a working roastery.

Named for family, Aunty Peg's is where Melbourne coffee gets roasted, packed, and sent around the country. Come in during wholesale hours or browse merch while the drums turn.
