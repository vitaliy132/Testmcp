Proud Mary was founded by husband and wife Nolan and Shari Hirte in 2009 in Melbourne, Australia. Proud Mary offers products and services that improve the start of your day so dramatically, that it's impossible to go back to the way things used to be.

We aim to provide an amazing and heartfelt experience for everyone who visits our cafe.

Always In Good Taste

We shouldn’t have to pick between good coffee and good food. Our cafes offer all-day breakfast, delicious lunch, high-end tea, fresh juice, smoothies, cakes, pastries, and table-service along with our coffee.

Great Coffee For All

We source high quality coffee direct from origin, roast the beans, and supply coffee around Australia and America. Through deep relationships with coffee farmers, we fight for more equitable practices and greater appreciation in the coffee space.
