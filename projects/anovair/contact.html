If you have any questions, please do not hesitate to contact us. We strive to respond within 24 hours. Customer service is open 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM, Monday to Friday. You can also write to info@anovair.com.

Frequently asked questions

How can I track my order?

Orders ship within 24–48 hours. You will receive a dispatch email with tracking details for our delivery partner.

How long will it take to receive my order?

UK / France / Australia: 4–7 days. Germany: 4–8. Netherlands / Austria / Poland: 6–8. Spain / Italy / Belgium / Canada: 5–8. USA: 7–14. Ireland: 7–13. Times start from dispatch.

What is your refund policy?

14 days from receipt to return without reason. Items must be unworn, unwashed and in original packaging with the hangtag uncut.

How do I make a return?

Use the Returns link under customer care, create your return, then follow the instructions our team sends.

How long will it take to receive a refund?

Within 10 working days after the parcel reaches our warehouse, then up to 3 days to appear on your statement.

Can I make an exchange?

Yes — start a return within 14 days and note the size or colour you want instead. We ship the replacement once we receive the original.

What payments methods can I use?

Visa, Mastercard, American Express, iDEAL, Bancontact, Sofort, PayPal, Apple Pay, Shop Pay, Google Pay, and Anovair gift cards.

Do you ship to my country?

We deliver worldwide with DHL, DPD, Royal Mail, UPS and local carriers. Free shipping over €150.

Will I be charged with VAT?

VAT is already included. There are no extra charges or duties upon delivery.
