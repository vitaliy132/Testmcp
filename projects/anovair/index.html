Frequently asked questions

Do you ship to my country?

We deliver worldwide with free shipping on orders over €150. Anovair orders are shipped via trusted carriers like DHL, DPD, Royal Mail, UPS and local services.

What is your refund policy?

We offer a 100% money-back guarantee. Customers have 14 days to return their packages without reason, starting from the date of receipt, except for Final Sale items.

What payments methods can I use?

Credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express), local methods including iDEAL, Bancontact and Sofort, PayPal, and mobile wallets such as Apple Pay, Shop Pay and Google Pay.

Will I be charged for customs duties?

No. VAT and customs duties are included in the prices listed. The price you see is the price you pay.
