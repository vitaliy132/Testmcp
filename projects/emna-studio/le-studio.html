Meet the founder

EMNA STUDIO, founded by Emna RACHED, is a creative architectural practice spearheaded by a Franco-Tunisian architect with over eight years of experience in the field. Currently based in Turkey, Emna has honed her expertise in the production of three-dimensional architectural imagery, having worked for a prominent Parisian agency known for its innovative approaches to architectural visualization. Throughout her career, she has collaborated with global architects on significant large-scale projects that have gained recognition in international competitions.

Emna's work is characterized by a meticulous attention to detail, which she believes is paramount to achieving coherence and integrity in architectural outcomes. Her artistic creations are deeply informed by her daily surroundings, drawing inspiration from both the intricacies of architectural elements and foundational geometrical forms. Emna's multicultural identity and diverse professional experiences converge to shape her unique artistic perspective, allowing her to cultivate an aesthetic that harmoniously merges minimalism with elements of orientalism.

Demonstrating acute sensitivity to her environment and the practical requirements of her clients, Emna aspires to create works that embody a seamless interplay between functionality, aesthetic purity, and the exquisite quality of artisanal craftsmanship. A salient aspect of her practice involves highlighting the Mediterranean lifestyle and honoring the skills of local artisans.

The studio — our mission

At EMNA STUDIO, we invite you to explore our innovative intersection of architecture and furniture design, where creativity and functionality coalesce to form unparalleled experiences for our clients. Our commitment to aesthetic excellence and practical utility is evident in every project we undertake. With a multidisciplinary team, we employ a forward-thinking methodology that challenges conventional boundaries, resulting in spaces and products that not only inspire but also enhance the quality of life.

We prioritize a holistic approach to client relationships, offering comprehensive support throughout each project and beyond. Our advisory services extend to the architectural evolution of properties as well as their real estate valuation.
